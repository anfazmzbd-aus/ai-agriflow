From data to decisions

How AgriFlow works

A coordinated agent workflow analyzes a question from several perspectives, then assembles a recommendation that people can understand and verify.

01 / ASK
💬

Describe a challenge

Start with a crop, location, season or agricultural decision.

02 / ANALYZE
🧩

Specialists collaborate

A planner routes work to relevant weather, soil, market, pest and knowledge agents.

03 / EVALUATE
🔎

Combine & check

Findings are fused, risks are assessed and confidence is checked.

04 / ACT
✅

Make an informed decision

Receive an understandable recommendation, with human approval where appropriate.

Conceptual end-to-end workflow — capabilities are being developed and validated as part of the AgriFlow capstone.

Intelligence by design

Multiple agents.
One coherent answer.

Instead of a one-size-fits-all chatbot, AgriFlow's planned architecture routes questions through a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of specialist agents. Parallel analysis, retrieval-augmented generation and a recommendation layer support traceable outputs.
